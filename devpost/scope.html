🎯 The Unique Kernel

Transforming messy brain dumps into an agent-ready build blueprint in one click.

Unlike generic chatbots that return walls of conversational text, Idea Breakdown Coach is purpose-built to bridge human brainstorming and AI coding agents. It outputs explicit build phases, concrete next steps, stack rationales, and exact agent prompting instructions.

🔄 The Core Loop

Relieving the stress: Replaces hours of tedious copying, pasting, and guessing with instant, structured clarity.
